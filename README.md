## 📌 About the Internship

The internship was completed under the AI Technology Team at Nine Triangles Digital Pvt. Ltd., with a focus on two connected ideas: **Digital Asset Management (DAM)** — organizing, tagging, storing, and retrieving an organization's digital content — and **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — an architecture that pairs a Large Language Model with a retrieval mechanism over an organization's own knowledge base so that AI-generated responses stay grounded in accurate, current information.

As documented on the internship completion certificate, the internship involved supporting the organization's AI technology initiatives through AI research, data organization, prompt engineering, testing of AI workflows, knowledge-base management, and evaluation of AI-powered features intended to enhance the performance and usability of the organization's technology platforms. The work was applied and evaluative in nature rather than product-development in nature, carried out under the supervision of the AI Technology Team.

---

## 🎯 Internship Objectives

- Understand, at a working level, how Digital Asset Management practices organize and govern an organization's digital content
- Study the principles of Retrieval-Augmented Generation and how retrieval and generation stages combine to produce grounded AI responses
- Assist in organizing data and digital assets so they are more easily discoverable and reusable
- Gain hands-on exposure to prompt engineering to improve the quality and reliability of AI-generated output
- Assist in testing AI workflows and identifying gaps between expected and actual system behaviour
- Support knowledge-base management activities that keep organizational information current and well-structured
- Assist in evaluating AI-powered features against usability and performance considerations, and document findings in a structured manner
- Develop a professional understanding of how an AI Technology team operates within a digital consulting organization

## 🧩 Technical Domains Covered

| Concept / Domain | Description |
|---|---|
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** | Broad field concerned with building systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence |
| **Large Language Models (LLMs)** | Models that generate natural-language responses; the generation stage of a RAG pipeline |
| **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** | Augmenting an LLM's prompt with content retrieved from an external knowledge base to improve factual grounding |
| **Digital Asset Management (DAM)** | Practices and systems for organizing, tagging, storing, and retrieving digital content |
| **Prompt Engineering** | Designing and refining input prompts to improve the accuracy and relevance of LLM output |
| **Knowledge Base Management** | Maintaining a structured, current repository of organizational information |
| **Data Organization / Tagging** | Structuring raw digital assets with consistent metadata, categories, and naming conventions |
| **AI Workflow Testing & Evaluation** | Systematically checking AI-powered features against expected behaviour, relevance, and usability |

> These are presented as general/conceptual technologies relevant to the domain, in line with the internship's applied and evaluative scope. No proprietary software products, datasets, or client-specific tools are named, as none are attested in the supporting documentation.
